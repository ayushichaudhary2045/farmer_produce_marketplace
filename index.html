How it works

Four steps from field to fair deal

No app to install, no broker to negotiate with — just a listing, a price you can trust, and a buyer on the other end.

01

List your produce

Add crop, quantity and your price — by form, or by a simple SMS if you're on a basic phone.

02

Compare the mandi price

See today's live Agmarknet rate and an AI-predicted price for next week, side by side with your asking price.

03

Buyers bid

Verified bulk buyers across India view your listing and place transparent bids.

04

Deal & alert

Accept a bid and get a WhatsApp or SMS alert the moment it happens — no missed offers.

For buyers

Browse today's listings

Every listing shows the farmer's asking price next to the live mandi rate, so you know it's fair before you bid.
